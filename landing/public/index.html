What it does

Keep typing claude

The sessions you already started, as they are. Nothing to relaunch, no tmux, no SSH, no port to open.

Every account, every machine

~/.claude and every ~/.claude-* on every PC, found automatically and merged into one list. Waiting approvals come first.

Notifications and approvals

A push when a turn ends or Claude needs you, on iPhone and Android. Approve, answer Claude's questions, or stop a running turn, right from your phone.

Auto-approve, on your terms

Let one session run unattended for 3 or 24 hours. It stays on screen the whole time, and one tap turns it off.

End-to-end encrypted

Your code and prompts are encrypted between your phone and your PC. The relay only passes ciphertext and connection metadata.

Open source, self-hostable

MIT licensed. Use it over Tailscale or run the relay in your own Cloudflare account: free, no account needed.

No app store needed

A web app you add to your home screen. Works on iPhone and Android today.

A cat for company

Pick one of seven cats. It runs while Claude is working.

Get started

On your PC (WSL / macOS / Linux, Node 24):

curl -fsSL https://app.nyan-remote.app/install.sh | bash

In a new terminal, sign in if you use our relay, then register your phone:

nyan login
nyan pair

On your phone, open app.nyan-remote.app, add it to your home screen, and scan the QR code.
