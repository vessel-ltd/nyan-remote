How it works

  1. Install on your PCOne command. WSL, macOS or Linux.
  2. Use claude as usualSame command, same accounts, nothing to relaunch.
  3. Open your phoneOpen a link and add it to your home screen, no app store. The sessions you start are all there, ready for you to answer and reply.

Why it is different

Your sessions, as they are

No wrapper command to start under, no tmux, no SSH. Nothing to prepare before you leave.

Every account, every machine

~/.claude and ~/.claude-<name> accounts on every PC, found automatically and merged into one list. Waiting approvals come first.

Open source and private

The agent on your PC, the web app and the relay are all open source (MIT) — read them on GitHub. Session content is end-to-end encrypted between your phone and your PC; the relay cannot read it. Or run it over Tailscale or your own relay.

On your phone

  • Notifications When a turn ends or Claude needs you, on iPhone and Android.
  • Approvals and questions Allow, deny, or pick an answer right there.
  • Reply and stop Type into a running session, or stop it.
  • Auto-approve, on your terms One session, for 3 or 24 hours. Always visible, one tap to turn off.
  • No app store A web app you add to your home screen.
  • A cat Pick one of seven. It runs while Claude is working.

Get started

On your PC (WSL / macOS / Linux, Node 24):

curl -fsSL https://app.nyan-remote.app/install.sh | bash

In a new terminal, sign in if you use our relay, then register your phone:

nyan login
nyan pair

On your phone, open app.nyan-remote.app, add it to your home screen, and scan the QR code.
